Connected accounts privacy

Connected accounts runs on your own Wizard server. You choose which Google accounts to connect and which apps may use them.

What is stored

Your Google app credentials and the access and refresh tokens for connected accounts are stored encrypted on your Wizard server. Your server administrator remains trusted. The account email address, connection status and approved permissions are shown in Connected accounts. Credentials and tokens are not shown in chat or returned to agent tools.

What access means

Google sign-in uses your email address to identify the account and can ask for read-only Calendar access. Tokens are used only for your approved Google requests, not shared with other people or AI chat. You must separately choose to show that account in the Calendar app. Optional YouTube catalog access is read-only and does not authorize audio playback. Connecting Google does not connect a real chat account or send Google data to the simulated private-summary demo.

Disconnecting

In Connected accounts, disconnect an account to remove local access and its dependent Calendar and Music permissions. Wizard asks Google to revoke the grant. If that cannot be confirmed, remove the app under your Google account’s third-party connections. You may remove Google permissions there at any time.

Your choices

You can stop Calendar using an account without disconnecting Google. You can unlink Music without removing another app’s permission. Your Google app is created in an account you own; your wizard does not need its own Google account.
